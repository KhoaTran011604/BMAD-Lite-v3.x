## Story 4.2: Low-Stock and Expiration Alerts
**As a** Farm Manager,
**I want** automated priority indicators for expiring or depleted materials,
**so that** I can purchase replacements before planting schedules are affected.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a material with stock level below its safety stock threshold, When the dashboard renders, Then it must display a "Low Stock Warning" with its shortage quantity and a distinct alert badge.
2. Given an imported batch expiring within 30 days of the current date, When the dashboard renders, Then it must trigger a "Near Expiry Alert" displaying the days remaining and batch code.
